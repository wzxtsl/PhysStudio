# Validation

Accepted simulations were produced on a shared NVIDIA RTX 3090 using Warp
1.6.2, NumPy 1.26.4 and SciPy 1.14.0. Shared GPU load makes these wall times
unsuitable for a paper performance comparison. Blender 4.2.9 Cycles renders
recorded states; it does not drive the simulation.

## Numerical checks

Run `python -m magnetic_rigid.validate --output outputs/magnetic_checks.json`
to reproduce the six numerical checks. The following measurements come from
the accepted showcase implementation.

| Check | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| Analytic magnetic force and offset/alignment torque vs Warp AD | maximum absolute gradient error 1.36e-20 |
| Element energy Hessian vs independent differences of AD gradients | maximum absolute error 1.75e-12 |
| Near/far transition | matching gradient, zero far-field Hessian |
| Isolated linear sphere induction | 0.014490804705882345 A m² vs analytic 0.014490804705882349 |
| Two moments of 50 A m², kappa 1e8, dt 0.01 s | final separation 0.08116057 m; minimum gap 0.00103105 m |
| Two moments of 500 A m², kappa 1e10, dt 0.002 s | final separation 0.08116053 m; minimum gap 0.00111152 m |
| Linear momentum in the two magnetic contact tests | errors below 4.8e-12 kg m/s |
| Sliding/rolling friction | kinetic energy drops from 0.003125 to 0.00223194 J; vx 0.17856 m/s, omega_y 4.46448 rad/s |
| Fast collision, opposing speeds 20 m/s, dt 0.02 s | no crossing; minimum gap 0.00096741 m; final kinetic energy 5.99e-6 J from 40 J |

Contact stiffness is scaled with magnetic strength in the stress checks. These
results do not imply automatic stable capture for arbitrary initial stiffness.
See [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) for the stiffness scaling and retry policy.

## Complete recordings

The audit recomputes geometric gaps for every saved frame, verifies all states
are finite, quaternion normalization, timestamps/frame counts and all accepted
Newton residuals. Full accepted-step diagnostics cover intervals between video
frames as well. No run accepts a nonconverged or penetrating state.

| Scene | Preset | Frames | Minimum substep gap | Maximum accepted residual | Refinement |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Compass garden | `compass_garden` | 301 | 0.0454668 m | 0.005790 | 1200 steps, no retry |
| Electromagnetic crane | `magnetic_crane` | 361 | 0.000297766 m | 0.199871 | 1440 steps, no retry |
| Magnetic globe | `magnetic_globe` | 361 | 0.000655694 m | 0.199446 | 1444 steps; four half-step refinements |

The presets are generated by `scenes.py`. Residuals use the
mass/inertia-scaled generalized acceleration criterion documented in the
solver. All scene tolerances are 0.2; kappa remains 1e8 throughout these runs.
The globe's four refinements occur during the release/drop around 9.5 seconds;
these are recorded, not silently skipped frames. Source hashes, configurations,
poses, velocities and moments accompany each recording.

The compass scene has 76 pivoted needles and a rotating source. The crane
contains 32 beads; up to 26 are airborne together. The globe contains 17 rigid
triads; up to 11 reach its upper hemisphere and all fall below z=0.144 m by the
end. The crane's visible guards match its side-contact planes, and its gantry
columns are outside the particle enclosure. The globe's inner collision radius
is 0.405 m; its visible glass shell uses that inner radius and 0.004 m thickness.

## Render and video acceptance

The three [showcase videos](../media/index.html#magnetic-rigid-bodies) contain
301, 361, and 361 frames at 1280 x 720 and 30 fps. Encoding verifies all expected
PNGs, H.264 frame counts, and full decoding. Posters and storyboards come from
the encoded video; the published MP4 hashes are in the
[media manifest](../media/manifest.json).

The compass rendering was resumed in nonoverlapping ranges with the same
saved states and global camera timing. A three-frame comparison after enabling
persistent scene caching and changing GPU found at most one 8-bit channel
level of difference (mean below 0.016), without a scene or camera change.

The public audit always checks finite states, quaternion normalization,
timestamps, contact gaps, and Newton residuals. Scene interactions are required
when the preset duration is complete; `--require-full-demo` also rejects short
recordings. A short CLI pipeline tests installation and execution, while the
complete scene audits establish the lift, alignment, and release behavior.

## Public interface validation

Each of the three presets completed a two-frame run through `python -m
physstudio run magnetic_rigid`, with the full body/sample resolution, a 320 x
180 Cycles render, physical audit, H.264 encoding and complete decoding.
The six numerical checks and all three full-duration scene audits pass using
the packaged code. Resuming a completed render preserves the existing PNGs.

The short runs retain the accepted configurations exactly apart from frame
count and device metadata. Crane/globe state prefixes match exactly. Compass
positions, moments, and time also match; maximum quaternion and angular-velocity
differences are 1.15e-13 and 3.76e-11 rad/s. The implementation and scene files
are unchanged, with only launcher and portable-path additions.

Nine frozen-state frames were rendered at 1280 x 720 and 32 samples and compared
with the accepted images. Mean absolute channel differences are below 0.018
on the 0–255 scale. Eight frames differ by at most one level; two channels in
the initial crane frame differ by two. All three published MP4s retain the
accepted SHA-256 hashes, and every frame of each GIF preview decodes.
